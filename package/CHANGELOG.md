## v1.0.2
- LookingGlass item stats for Hex of Rain, Strife, Ossification and Greed now describe the HexTweaks effects instead of the vanilla ones (for example, Strife no longer shows "Extra Shrine of the Mountain Stacks"). Only applies when LookingGlass is installed.
- Hex of Strife now makes the holder deal 25% less damage to bosses per stack by default (was 20%), so it stands apart from Hex of Rain. Existing configs keep their current value.
